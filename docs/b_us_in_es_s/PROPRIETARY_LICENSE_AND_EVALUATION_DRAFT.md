# DGTL Proprietary License And Evaluation Draft

This file is a business draft, not legal advice. It is intended to help define the commercial posture of DGTL before final legal review.

## Recommended Licensing Direction

Recommended default position for DGTL:

- proprietary
- source-controlled distribution
- paid commercial or evaluation access
- no open-source redistribution rights by default

## Draft Repository Notice

Use wording like the following until formal legal text is finalized:

```text
Copyright (c) 2026 DGTL.
All rights reserved.

This software and associated documentation files are proprietary and confidential.
No part of this software may be used, copied, modified, distributed, sublicensed,
published, or disclosed without prior written permission from the copyright holder,
except where explicitly allowed by a signed commercial or evaluation agreement.
```

## Draft Commercial License Position

A commercial production license should define:

- who is licensed
- whether access is source, binary, or both
- which products or internal systems may use DGTL
- whether redistribution is allowed
- support and update scope
- term, renewal, and termination conditions

## Draft Commercial License Summary

Suggested high-level terms:

- internal commercial use allowed only under written agreement
- no resale, sublicensing, redistribution, or OEM bundling unless explicitly granted
- no public disclosure of source or private implementation details
- updates and support only for the agreement period
- benchmark publication only with written approval if needed

## Draft Evaluation License Position

Evaluation access should be narrower than production rights.

Suggested evaluation restrictions:

- evaluation use only
- no production deployment
- no redistribution
- no sublicensing
- no public benchmark publication without permission
- fixed duration
- automatic expiry unless extended or converted

## Draft Evaluation Terms Summary

Suggested short-form evaluation wording:

```text
The evaluator is granted a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable,
revocable right to use DGTL solely for internal evaluation purposes during
the evaluation period. Production use, redistribution, sublicensing,
public hosting, resale, or external transfer are prohibited unless separately
authorized in writing.
```

## Recommended Agreement Components

For customer-facing agreements, define these sections clearly:

- definitions
- scope of license
- evaluation vs production use
- redistribution restrictions
- confidentiality
- support and maintenance
- fees and payment terms
- intellectual property ownership
- disclaimer of warranties
- limitation of liability
- termination and post-termination obligations

## Suggested Commercial Offers

### Evaluation Agreement

- short term
- limited support
- limited usage rights
- designed to convert to commercial license

### Commercial License

- annual internal-use license
- standard support and updates
- optional add-on services

### Enterprise Agreement

- negotiated deployment scope
- custom support SLA
- custom engineering and integration support
- roadmap collaboration

## What To Avoid Before Legal Review

Do not promise:

- perpetual unrestricted reuse
- redistribution rights by default
- unlimited support
- patent grants unless intentionally reviewed and approved
