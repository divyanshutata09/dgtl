## Section: The Problem

Your team may already know how to build a frontend. The expensive part comes after that:

- backend engineering
- optimization passes
- target support
- release validation
- deterministic regression control

Most teams do not want to build and maintain that stack internally.

## Section: What DGTL Does

DGTL is a commercial backend and optimization engine for teams building:

- internal DSLs
- runtime execution systems
- quant and low-latency infrastructure
- AI lowering pipelines
- specialized embedded or edge toolchains

DGTL lets you:

- lower frontend output into DGTL IR
- run deterministic optimization and validation workflows
- emit production-oriented output for ARM64, x64, NASM, WASM, ELF .o, COFF .obj/.lib, and Mach-O
