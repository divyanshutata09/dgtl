# DGTL One-Page Sales Deck

## Headline

Commercial multi-target backend infrastructure for custom languages and performance-critical runtimes.

## Subheadline

Lower your frontend into DGTL IR, apply deterministic optimization passes, and emit production-ready output across ARM64, x64, NASM, WASM, native object files, and static libraries without building a backend stack from scratch.

## What DGTL Solves

Teams building DSLs, execution engines, or specialized runtimes often solve the frontend first and then hit the expensive part:

- backend engineering
- target support
- optimization passes
- reproducible validation

DGTL exists to remove that bottleneck.
