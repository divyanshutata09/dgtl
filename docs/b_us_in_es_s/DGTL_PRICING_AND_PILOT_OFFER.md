# DGTL Pricing And Pilot Offer

This document turns the DGTL business model into a concrete commercial offer structure.

## Pricing Philosophy

DGTL should be sold on engineering value, delivery speed, and backend expertise, not as a low-cost commodity library.

Early pricing should qualify serious buyers and preserve room for enterprise expansion.

## 1. Paid Pilot Offer

### Pilot Package

- Duration: 6 to 8 weeks
- Price: USD 15,000 to USD 30,000
- Scope: one customer team, one representative workload, one primary target backend

### Pilot Deliverables

- evaluation access to DGTL
- onboarding session
- frontend-to-DGTL integration review
- one target/backend validation flow
- one written findings and next-steps summary

### Pilot Success Criteria

- customer can lower one meaningful workload into DGTL IR
- DGTL emits the agreed target output successfully
- conformance and smoke workflow run reproducibly
- customer sees enough value to justify commercial conversion

### Pilot Exclusions

- no unrestricted redistribution rights
- no broad production deployment rights
- no unlimited custom engineering
- no SLA beyond pilot scope

## 2. Commercial License

### Commercial Tier

- Price: USD 25,000 to USD 75,000 per year
- Intended for: internal production use by one organization

### Includes

- production internal-use rights
- source access under commercial terms
- standard updates during active term
- standard support window
- limited advisory support

### Best For

- teams with a working pilot
- internal DSL or tooling teams
- organizations needing production use without enterprise customization

## 3. Enterprise License

### Enterprise Tier

- Price: starts at USD 100,000 per year
- Intended for: high-value deployments needing support, roadmap influence, or custom work

### Includes

- commercial production rights
- priority support
- custom engineering block or negotiated services
- target bring-up or backend customization options
- release and validation collaboration

### Best For

- HFT / quant infrastructure
- AI backend/runtime vendors
- embedded or edge deployment teams
- organizations with platform-specific needs

## 4. Custom Engineering

### Consulting / Integration Services

- Rate: USD 250 to USD 400 per hour
or
- Fixed-scope engagement: USD 10,000+ depending on scope

### Typical Service Work

- AST-to-IR lowering help
- backend customization
- optimization pass tuning
- performance investigation
- release and regression validation

## Recommended Launch Offer

For first commercial outreach, sell this package first:

### DGTL Pilot Launch Offer

- Price: USD 20,000 fixed
- Duration: 6 weeks
- Includes:
  - one onboarding workshop
  - one target backend focus
  - one representative customer workload
  - one conformance and validation setup
  - one final technical recommendation document

### Conversion Path

- convert to Commercial if the customer wants internal production use
- convert to Enterprise if the customer needs priority support or custom backend work

## Discount Guidance

- avoid discounting the first pilot below strategic qualification level
- discount only in exchange for a strong logo, testimonial, or deeper follow-on scope
- do not position DGTL as cheap infrastructure

## Commercial Packaging Summary

### Pilot

- USD 15,000 to USD 30,000
- short-term validation
- limited scope

### Commercial

- USD 25,000 to USD 75,000 annually
- internal production use
- standard support

### Enterprise

- USD 100,000+ annually
- custom support and engineering
- strategic deployment support

## Negotiation Guardrails

- keep redistribution rights separate and premium-priced
- keep OEM or sublicensing rights separate and explicitly negotiated
- do not include unlimited support by default
- do not bundle extensive custom engineering into base license price
